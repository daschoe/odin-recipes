Ramen

Ramen is a nice and quick recipe for lunch, that is also highly customizable. however, the presented recipe does not claim to be traditional.

Ingredients (per portion)

  • 500ml water
  • 1tsp vegetable broth
  • 1 carrot
  • 100g shiitake mushrooms
  • additional vegetables of choice such as sweetcorn, edamame, broccoli, pakchoi ...
  • 50g ramen noodles
  • 50g somen (buckwheat) noodles
  • 1tsp miso paste
  • some oil for frying
  • one block of tofu
  • 1tsp sugar
  • 1tsp chilli flakes
  • Sesame seeds
This yields two servings.

Steps

  1. Combine water and broth in a pot and bring to boil.
  2. Wash and slice the carrot and add it to the pot first.
  3. Remove the ends of the shiitake stems. Slice the mushrooms and add them as well.
  4. Repeat for the rest of the vegetables as suited.
  5. Add both types of noodles to the pot.
  6. Preheat a pan with oil to medium heat.
  7. slice the tofu into 5-10mm pieces and add them to the pan.
  8. Sprinkle sugar and chilli flakes onto the tofu and fry from both sides.
  9. In the meantime remove the pot from heat and stir in the miso paste until dissolved.
  10. Serve in a bowl together with the fried tofu and toppings of your choice such as sesame seeds or sliced spring onions.
