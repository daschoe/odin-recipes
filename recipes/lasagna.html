Lasagna

Lasagna can also be made in vegetarian.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 package of lasagna plates
  • 1 can of crushed tomatoes
  • 1 zucchini
  • 3-5 cloves of garlic
  • 1-2tbls olive oil
  • spices such as oregano, basil, rosemary, thyme, chilli flakes, salt, pepper to your liking
  • 1 pack of grated cheese
This yields two to four portions.

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Mince the garlic.
  3. Heat the oil in a pot and add the garlic.
  4. Grate the zucchini into the pot.
  5. Add tomato sauce to the pot.
  6. Stir in the spices.
  7. Line a large oven-proof bowl with lasagna sheets.
  8. Add 1-3 heaping spoons of the sauce, spread it and cover with more lasagna sheets. Repeat until no sauce remains.
  9. Top the upmost lasagna layer evenly with grated cheese.
  10. Cook in oven for approximately 20 minutes.
