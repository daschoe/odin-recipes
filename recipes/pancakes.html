Pancakes

Pancakes are a all-time favorite food in childrens' stories such as the Moomins, Petzi, or Petterson. Beware that in Germany "Pfannkuchen" can mean different things depending on the location you are in.

Ingredients (per portion)

  • 1 egg
  • 80g flour
  • 140ml milk
    • Enjoy with jam, chocolate, sugar or whatever suits your fancy
  • a pinch of salt
  • oil for frying
This yields two pancakes.

Steps

  1. Mix together flour, milk and salt until all lumps have disappeared.
  2. Add the egg to the mixture and whisk again until combined.
  3. In the meantime preheat a pan with a bit of oil to medium heat (5-6/9).
  4. Pour half of the batter into the pan and fry from both sides until golden brown.
  5. Repeat the last step with the remaining batter.
  6. Enjoy with jam, chocolate, sugar or whatever suits your fancy.
